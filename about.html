About

The Finance Lawyer is the personal blog of Russell Stanley Q. Geronimo, who is a corporate and finance lawyer and the Founder and Managing Lawyer of Geronimo Law. He is also the Founder and CEO of Strago, a finance and consulting firm, and a Co-Founder of Mage Partners, a management consulting firm.

His practice covers mergers and acquisitions, banking and finance, securities and investments, venture capital and private equity, public-private partnerships, project finance, energy and infrastructure.

Before founding the firm, he was an associate at SyCip Salazar Hernandez & Gatmaitan, a Court Attorney at the Supreme Court of the Philippines, and a Division Chief at the Governance Commission for GOCCs under the Office of the President. He has also served as a consultant to the World Bank, The Asia Foundation, the PPP Center, the Senate and the House of Representatives.

He teaches law at the University of the Philippines College of Law and the De La Salle University College of Law, among other law schools, and finance at the De La Salle University College of Business. He is the author of Law and Finance (2019), and he holds a Juris Doctor from the University of the Philippines College of Law and a finance degree from De La Salle University.

The articles express his personal views and are not legal advice.
