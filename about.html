The Finance Lawyer is a collection of essays on banking, finance, capital markets and securities regulation, written for lawyers, bankers, founders and students who want to understand how financial transactions are put together and regulated.

It is written by Russell Stanley Q. Geronimo, Founder and Managing Lawyer of Geronimo Law in Makati City and the author of Law and Finance.

The essays express the personal views of the author and are not legal advice.
